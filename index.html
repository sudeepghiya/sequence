♠ Sequence ♥

Play the card-and-chip game with friends anywhere.

How to play
  • Tap a card in your hand, then tap a highlighted space on the board to place your chip. Each card appears twice on the board.
  • Get 5 chips in a row (across, down or diagonal) to make a sequence. The ★ corners count for everyone.
  • 2 teams need 2 sequences to win. 3 teams need 1.
  • Two-eyed Jacks (♦ ♣) are wild: place a chip on any open space.
  • One-eyed Jacks (♠ ♥) remove an opponent's chip (not one in a finished sequence).
  • If both spaces for a card are covered, it's dead: swap it for a new card (once per turn), then play.
